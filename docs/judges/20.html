Morpheme colours
Colour = morpheme function. Warm (red → orange → wheat, in word order) marks VERB machinery; violet + teal mark NOUN endings; the three glued particles each have their own hue — grape vav, lemon article, ice preposition; spring-green marks an attached pronoun. Pink & blue are reserved for the gender layer.

Binyan (verbal stem)
The line IS the grammar table. Position = voice: line BELOW = active, ABOVE = passive, BOTH sides = reflexive. Count = agency: ONE line = simple, TWO = the dagesh-doubled intensives, THREE = causative (“Qal + Piel, but even moreso — it causes”). Copper = active, slate = passive.
Qalsimple active — one line below
Nifalsimple passive — Qal’s mirror, one line above
Pielintensive active — DOUBLE line = the dagesh
Pualintensive passive — Piel’s mirror
Hifilcausative active — THREE lines below
Hofalcausative passive — three lines above
Hitpaelreflexive — doubled lines BOTH sides (action returns to subject)
Qal passiveqatul participle — passive by vowels only, so a dotted line
Minor and Aramaic stems wear their FAMILY’s mark (Polel = Piel, Hafel = Hifil, Hitpolel = Hitpael…); the panel names the exact stem. Internalize the marks and you have memorized the stem chart.
